**Subject:** Permission request: derived statistics from public results in a non-commercial portfolio project

Hello,

I'm a software engineer building a personal, non-commercial portfolio project called
Trackside. It is a small web app that follows the GT3 season and shows, for each circuit,
which car models have been quick there in previous years.

To do that I would like to use the session result files you publish for the IMSA WeatherTech
Championship (GTD and GTD Pro classes) and GT World Challenge America, specifically the
results and time card CSVs.

I've read the notice on your results pages that the data is owned by Al Kamel Systems and may
not be distributed without consent, so I'm asking before I publish anything. What I propose:

- The raw files are stored privately and are never republished or offered for download.
- The public site shows only derived aggregates, such as best and average lap by car model
  per circuit and gaps between models.
- Al Kamel Systems is credited as the timing data source on every page that shows these
  figures.
- Files are fetched once per session after publication, cached, and rate-limited.
- The project has no advertising, no paid access, and no commercial use.

Would you be willing to grant permission for this use? If you have conditions, such as
specific attribution wording or limits on what can be shown, I'm glad to follow them. If the
answer is no, I'll use other data.
